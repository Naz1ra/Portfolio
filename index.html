AgroSense

AI smart agriculture system · Final Year Project · team of 3
+

An IoT irrigation system that stops overwatering, underwatering and water waste caused by manual checks and timer-based watering.
ESP32-S3IoTWeb dashboardAI logic
  • Designed the hardware around an ESP32-S3 with soil moisture, DHT22 temperature and humidity, and water level sensors.
  • Planned Wi-Fi transmission of sensor data to a web dashboard so farmers and home gardeners can monitor remotely.
  • Designed automatic pump control through a relay module, with adjustable moisture thresholds and watering intervals.

  • Defined a testing plan for sensor accuracy, irrigation triggering, data transmission reliability and dashboard usability.
